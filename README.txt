GALLERY OF EPISTLES

1. Open index.html to view the gallery.
2. Put your photos in the images folder.
3. Edit epistles.js: set each image path, title, sender, date, and letter.
4. Save the files and refresh index.html.

Duplicate an entry in epistles.js to add another image and letter. Click an image in the gallery to read the letter behind it.

Controls
- Laptop keyboard: WASD to move, Q/E to rise or dip. Drag to look around.
- Laptop trackpad: two-finger scroll to move forward/back, horizontal scroll to strafe, and Alt/Option plus scroll to rise/dip. Drag to look around.
- Phone/tablet: use the on-screen direction pad and Rise/Dip buttons. Drag to look around.
